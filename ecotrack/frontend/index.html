About EcoTrack

Understanding our approach, technology, and estimation science

🌱

Why Everyday Habits Matter

Large global environmental challenges often feel overwhelming. However, individual habits—such as refilling a reusable bottle, taking the bus, or separating compostable waste—collectively divert thousands of pounds of trash and significantly lower daily fossil fuel consumption.

🎯

How EcoTrack Helps You

EcoTrack provides a straightforward, zero-friction interface for students and individuals to log daily sustainable choices. By viewing real-time streaks and aggregated metrics, users stay motivated and develop permanent environmentally responsible routines.

📊

Transparent Impact Estimates

Our calculations are powered by a dedicated Python Flask calculation microservice using published benchmarks from the US EPA WARM and UK DEFRA life-cycle models. We treat every habit uniquely and clearly label all figures as educational estimates rather than certified carbon offsets.
